# What to give the skill

The more of this you provide, the fewer checks come back as "unknown". You do not need all of it. One export from one platform is enough to start.

Always include, in your own words: business type, monthly spend by platform, your goal (purchases, leads, installs, calls), your target CPA or ROAS, and the date range with a comparison period.

## Google Ads

- Campaign, ad group, and keyword exports for the period (Reports, then download as CSV).
- Search Terms report for the same period.
- Screenshot of Goals, then Conversions, showing each action, its category, and whether it is Primary or Secondary.
- Screenshot of any campaign's bidding section, so the skill can see the strategy and target.
- If you run PMax: asset group list with asset strength, and the Insights tab for search categories.
- Change History for the period if performance moved and you want to know why.
- Consent Mode status from Tag Manager or the Diagnostics tab.

## Meta Ads

- Ads Manager export at ad level with these columns: Amount spent, Impressions, Reach, Frequency, CTR (all and link), CPM, Results, Cost per result, Purchase ROAS if relevant, Video plays at 25/50/75/100 if relevant.
- The same export at ad set level, so the skill can see budgets and audiences.
- Events Manager screenshot showing the pixel, CAPI status, and Event Match Quality per event.
- Attribution setting used for the export (for example 7-day click, 1-day view).
- Count of distinct creatives that ran, or the creative export.
- Any Special Ad Category declared.

## YouTube

- Comes through the Google Ads export. Add a video campaign export with View rate, CPV, and Watch time columns.
- Note which formats ran: skippable in-stream, bumper, Shorts, in-feed.

## LinkedIn Ads

- Campaign Manager export at campaign and ad level.
- Insight Tag status and any conversions defined.
- Lead gen form completion rate if you use forms.
- Audience size per campaign.

## TikTok Ads

- Ads Manager export at ad group and ad level, with 2-second and 6-second view rates.
- Pixel and Events API status screenshot.
- Whether creatives have sound and captions.
- Smart+ or Shop campaign use.

## Microsoft Ads

- Campaign and keyword export.
- UET tag status.
- Whether the account is an import from Google and when it was last synced.

## Apple Ads

- Campaign and keyword export with Tap-through rate, CPT, CPA, and Installs.
- AdAttributionKit or SKAdNetwork setup notes.

## Landing pages

- URLs for the top three landing pages by spend.
- Page speed score if you have it.

## Commerce and analytics

Optional, but it lets the skill compare platform-reported revenue with what actually happened: Shopify or store orders and refunds for the period, and analytics sessions by source.
